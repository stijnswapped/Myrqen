# Preliminary name screening

## Important

This is not legal clearance, trademark advice, or a guarantee that a name is unregistered. Search results can miss national marks, pending applications, unindexed companies, common-law rights, domains, and confusingly similar names. Before launch, perform a professional clearance search in intended markets and obtain legal advice.

## Working codename

`Myrqen` currently had the cleanest exact software/security collision profile among the generated candidates during a preliminary August 2026 web search. Searches surfaced adjacent names/marks, so it remains a codename only.

## Rejected/avoided candidates from the preliminary screen

Examples rejected because an exact or close software/company/product/trademark collision appeared: Varnyx, Nyrvex, Qevra, Oryvex, Vynqor, Senvyx, Tarnyx, Noxvra, Aqryn, TivRen, Verqis, Kenvra, Telvra, Kervyx, Nyrven, Vornyx, Zenvor, Krovyn, Deyron, Tekryn, Delqen, Kavqen, Zevqor, and others.

Do not assume a rejected name remains unavailable forever; this list exists to prevent repeating the same brainstorming work.

## Required launch clearance

At minimum search:
- WIPO Global Brand Database;
- EUIPO for EU marks;
- BOIP/Benelux if relevant;
- USPTO if US launch/marketing is planned;
- relevant national company registers;
- search engines and developer ecosystems (npm, PyPI, GitHub, package registries);
- relevant domains/social handles;
- confusingly similar phonetic/spelling marks, not only exact strings.

Use counsel for final likelihood-of-confusion analysis and filing strategy.
