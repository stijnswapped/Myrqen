# Entitlement boundaries

Pricing is intentionally postponed. Build entitlement checks so plans can change without changing scan/report contracts.

## Free
- scanning and local results;
- local exports;
- 5 cloud reports per quota day;
- 15 cloud reports per quota week;
- 21-day cloud retention;
- basic share controls required by POC;
- device and account management.

## Pro - architecture target
- longer or configurable individual retention;
- richer history/search;
- advanced personal workflow features;
- possibly additional sync allowance, never paywalling local findings.

## Team - architecture target
- team workspaces and collaboration at commercial scale;
- shared policies;
- membership/role administration;
- longer retention;
- advanced audit, exports, and centralized controls.

## Enterprise - architecture target
- SSO/SCIM;
- organization-level audit/compliance;
- custom retention/residency controls;
- contractual support and administration.

## Rule

Never make the finding body or remediation itself "paid content" after a free local scan. Monetize cloud/service value rather than withholding a vulnerability already discovered on the user's machine.
